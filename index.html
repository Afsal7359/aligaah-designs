The Aligaah story

Designed in-house.
Finished by hand.

Aligaah Designs began with a single loom and a simple belief — that everyday ethnic wear deserves the same care as bridal couture. Today, our small team designs every saree, kurti and churidar in-house, sources fabric directly from weavers, and checks each piece by hand before it leaves the studio.

The peacock feather in our emblem is a promise: colour that lasts, and grace in every drape.

— The Aligaah family
